Our Story

Born from a love of
pure, purposeful design.

Aquafold was born from a simple but frustrating truth — the world is drowning in plastic, and the so-called eco-friendly alternatives were quietly hiding plastic liners inside. We refused to accept that performance and sustainability had to be a trade-off. Our answer came from two of nature's most overlooked materials — agro-based fiber waste that gets discarded across India every single day, and a plant-based barrier coating that resists liquids and grease exactly like plastic does, but biodegrades completely in soil, compost.

We brought them together into one product — packaging that is strong, food-safe, and genuinely, uncompromisingly plastic-free from the outside in. Not mostly plastic-free. Not plastic-free on the label only. Every single layer. Aquafold is proof that doing the right thing and doing it well are not opposites.

Deep Science

The Technology Behind Every Layer

Two materials. One breakthrough process. Here is the full picture of how Aquafold engineers packaging that performs like plastic but disappears like nature intended.

🌾
The Shell: Agro-Based Fiber
We source agricultural fiber waste from across India — sugarcane bagasse, wheat straw, rice husk — materials that are otherwise burned or discarded. This fiber is cleaned, refined, and processed into the structural outer shell of every Aquafold product.
✦Sourced from agricultural byproduct streams across India
✦No virgin tree pulp or virgin plastic used
✦Molded under heat and pressure for structural integrity
🧬
The Barrier: Plant-Based Coating
The inner barrier coating is the heart of our innovation. A thin layer of plant-derived material is applied to the interior surface — creating a seamless shield that blocks liquids and grease exactly as well as plastic does, but biodegrades completely in compost and soil conditions.
✦No petroleum-derived polymers anywhere in the formulation
✦Grease and liquid resistant up to 100°C
✦Certified for direct food contact safety
The Process

From field waste to food-safe packaging — in four steps.

🔬
Step 01
Collection & Sourcing
Agro-fiber waste is collected from farms and processing plants across India, cleaned and quality-graded.
⚡
Step 02
Pulping & Molding
Fiber is turned into pulp and molded under heat and pressure into precise product shapes with tight dimensional tolerances.
🛡️
Step 03
Barrier Application
Our plant-based barrier coating is applied to the interior surface — sealing the fiber and creating liquid and grease resistance without any plastic.
🧪
Step 04
Testing & Certification
Every batch is tested for food contact safety, structural integrity, and biodegradation performance before leaving our facility.
