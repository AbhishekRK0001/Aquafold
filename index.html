Our Story

Born from a love of
pure, purposeful design.

Aquafold was born from a simple but frustrating truth — the world is drowning in plastic, and the so-called eco-friendly alternatives were quietly hiding plastic liners inside. We refused to accept that performance and sustainability had to be a trade-off. Our answer came from two of nature's most overlooked materials — agro-based fiber waste that gets discarded across India every single day, and a plant-based barrier coating that resists liquids and grease exactly like plastic does, but biodegrades completely in soil, compost.

We brought them together into one product — packaging that is strong, food-safe, and genuinely, uncompromisingly plastic-free from the outside in. Not mostly plastic-free. Not plastic-free on the label only. Every single layer. Aquafold is proof that doing the right thing and doing it well are not opposites.

Our Technology

How we make it — nature does most of the work.

Every step is driven by nature, not chemistry. Here is how we build packaging that works in the real world and disappears responsibly afterward.

🔬
Raw Material Collection
Agro-based fiber waste is collected, cleaned, and refined — giving agricultural byproducts a purposeful second life instead of being discarded.
⚡
Pulping & Molding
The fiber is converted into pulp and molded under heat and pressure to form the product shape.
🛡️
Barrier Coating
A thin plant-based barrier coating is applied to the interior — creating a seamless layer that resists liquids and grease completely.
🧪
Testing & Biodegradation
Products are tested for quality and safely decompose back into the environment after use.
Sustainability

Our commitment — no shortcuts, no exceptions.

We do not use the word sustainable lightly. Every claim we make is backed by material certifications and transparent sourcing. We will never put a green label on something that does not deserve it.

🌾
Built From Agricultural Waste
We use agro-based fiber waste that would otherwise be discarded or burned — turning an agricultural problem into a packaging solution.
🌱
Plant-Based All The Way Through
Both the outer shell and the inner barrier coating are made from plant-based materials. No synthetic chemicals, no petroleum-derived ingredients anywhere in the product.
♻️
Designed to Compost Completely
Aquafold packaging is designed to break down after use — in soil and compost conditions — leaving nothing harmful behind. No microplastics, no residue.
🤝
Honest at Every Stage
We are in development and we will never make claims we cannot back up. As we grow, our commitments will grow with us — transparently and honestly.
