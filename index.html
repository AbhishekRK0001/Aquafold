Our Story

Born from a love of
pure, purposeful design.

Aquafold was born from a simple but frustrating truth — the world was drowning in plastic, and the so-called eco-friendly alternatives were quietly hiding plastic liners inside. We refused to accept that performance and sustainability had to be a trade-off. Our answer came from two of nature's most overlooked materials — bagasse, the fibrous sugarcane waste that gets burned across India every single day, and naturally occurring biopolymers that barriers liquids and grease exactly like plastic does, but biodegrades completely in soil, compost, and ocean.

We brought them together into one product — a packaging that is strong, food-safe, and genuinely, uncompromisingly plastic-free from the outside in. Aquafold is not just packaging. It is proof that doing the right thing and doing it well are not opposites.

Our Technology

Science you can taste the difference in.

We don't just make products. We engineer ecosystems. Every product is a marvel of material science, nano-engineering, and smart design.

Raw Material Collection
Bagasse (sugarcane fiber waste) is collected and cleaned to prepare it for processing.

Pulping & Molding
The fiber is converted into pulp and molded under heat and pressure to form the product shape.
