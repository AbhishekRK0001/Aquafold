Features

Packaging engineered for real-world performance.

Aquafold packaging is designed around the operational realities of delivery, food service, and high-volume handling. Each material decision is driven by performance, scalability, and environmental direction.

See Technology Explore Products
Material System

Made from agricultural fiber instead of fossil-based plastic.

Plant-Based Material Composition

Aquafold packaging begins with bagasse and molded fiber substrates that provide structure, durability, and renewable sourcing potential.

  • Uses agricultural byproduct feedstock
  • No virgin plastic structure required
  • Supports circular manufacturing direction
  • Compatible with scalable molding workflows
Leak Resistance

Built to survive delivery conditions and liquid-heavy food applications.

Food delivery environments create stress through moisture, stacking, transport vibration, and holding time. Aquafold is designed to address these operational conditions.

Performance-Oriented Barrier Direction

Bio-based barrier coating direction improves resistance against oils, gravies, sauces, and liquid exposure.

  • Improved moisture handling
  • Reduced seepage risk
  • Designed for real delivery cycles
  • Supports stacked transport
Gallery Space

Dedicated visual zones for product, manufacturing, and material storytelling.

Compostable Direction

Built to exit the waste stream differently.

Packaging is designed with end-of-life intent in mind, prioritizing materials that biodegrade under composting conditions rather than persisting for decades.

Environmental Design Philosophy

Functional lifespan should be measured in hours while waste persistence should be minimized wherever possible.

Manufacturing

Designed with scalability in mind.

Sustainable packaging only matters when it can be manufactured consistently, efficiently, and at realistic commercial volumes.
